## Notes on the edit

Deliberately short — about 75 words. An earlier draft ran ~180 and restated the page's
proof (33 signups in 65 hours, 12/13 checks, the 2h21m session, a paragraph enumerating
the failures). All of that is on the build page, substantiated, where a reader who
clicked actually wants it. The email's job is the click, not the argument.

One line was cut for **accuracy**, not length: *"If you got anything odd from me around
29 July, that's why."* It doesn't apply to anyone who can receive this. Confirmed
subscribers got a confirmation email they asked for; the people who got something odd
were the 33 strangers in the spam wave — and those are precisely the people who must not
be emailed, since emailing them again would repeat the harm the build is about.

Kept: the "form you used" hook, because it's specific and true for the recipient; one
honesty signal, because that's the differentiator; and a closing line saying what the
list actually is, which is what a first issue most needs to do.
