# Public Build Thesis — reusable template for selected product experiments

**Status:** DRAFT until founder-reviewed. This is a public-facing explanation of why a build is worth testing, **not** a copy of confidential research.

## Observation
What practical buyer workflow or recurring decision caught our attention? Distinguish general logic, firsthand experience, original public evidence, and things still unknown. Do not imply buyer interviews or measured losses that did not occur.

## Why the job matters
What outcome might improve for a real user? Explain the possible value without inventing ROI, market size or willingness to pay.

## Existing alternatives
What do incumbent products and the customer's existing processes already do? Do not fabricate an unsolved gap or misleadingly frame basic features as novel.

## Testable hypothesis
What narrower claim would make a new product worthwhile *if validated*? Explain the proposed differentiator as an uncertainty, not an accomplished feature.

## Minimum experiment
Why this small build rather than full software? What observable action or real conversation will test the important uncertainty? Define the boundary between an engineering success and buyer validation.

## Falsifiers and pivot choices
What outcomes would lead to GO / REVISE / PARK / KILL, including a finding that the customer's existing tools already suffice? Record meaningful negative evidence as carefully as successes.

## Engineering execution and outcomes
Point to build-notes, tests, implementation PR/preview and actual observed outcomes as they become available. Do not write them before they happen.

## Public / private boundary
Include only founder-reviewed, publishable product reasoning. Do not publish confidential sourcing, independent bot exchanges, internal rankings, reports, private repo links, buyer identities or strategy. A selected experiment can be justified without exposing how it was discovered. Do not imply every project requires a product experiment; ordinary site maintenance and private client work follow their own processes.

**Release gate:** Founder approves the public rationale and eventual build article. The landing page speaks to prospective customers; the Jigg.AI build journal explains product judgment and implementation. Never auto-publish this template or treat it as a substitute for buyer evidence.
