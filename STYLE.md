# STYLE — voice for everything Jigg.AI publishes

Two surfaces, two voices (CONTEXT §4). The rules under "Both" apply everywhere.

## The journal — build logs
- First person, as Jigg.AI-the-builder — a consistent brand persona. The About page
  explains that a human directs the AI tools.
- Honest over polished. Show the mistakes, retries, and dead ends — that's what
  makes "anyone can build real things with AI" credible.
- Lead with a self-contained summary (the citation hook): what it is, whether it's
  worth it, the one caveat.
- Fair to tools: separate tool limitations from my own setup mistakes.
- Affiliate links always disclosed, and placed after the proof.
- Say "an AI tool worth your time" — never "popular".
- No selling inside a build log. The only path to the studio is the standard line at
  the end of the page.

## Studio pages — home, Services, About, Contact
- "Jigg.AI" is the subject: "Jigg.AI builds…", not "we build…". Never a "we" that
  suggests a team that doesn't exist. Say plainly that clients work directly with the
  founder.
- Name the founder only where accountability matters (CONTEXT §4), using the verified
  credential line and a LinkedIn link. No résumé, no biography.
- Capabilities, not hype. No generic AI marketing, no invented achievements, no client
  logos or outcomes without written permission, and never name a prospective client.
- Every service names its evidence. An internal build is a demonstration, not a client
  engagement; don't let the copy blur the two.
- Wherever a studio page mentions building in public, say whose work that is: the
  journal is Jigg.AI's own projects, and client work is private by default, shared only
  with the client's written permission. A prospective client should never come away
  thinking their project will be published.
- No affiliate links on studio pages.

## Both
- Concrete proof over adjectives: times, costs, test scores, screenshots. Speed and
  cost claims come only from recorded numbers (CONTEXT §5), never from impressions.
- Human-directed, AI-executed — never "autonomous".
- Don't claim the approach is new. Human–AI collaboration isn't Jigg.AI's invention; the
  difference is how openly and how well it's done, and the evidence for that is the build
  logs, not words like "unique" or "first".
- Plain naming: "Builds", "Tools", "Services" — no forced "jig" wordplay.
- Brand name: always "Jigg.AI", never bare "Jigg".
