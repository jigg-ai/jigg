Modernization is the work organizations keep postponing: the system still runs the business, nobody wants to break it, and the people who could change it are busy keeping it running.

Existing systems carry decisions nobody wrote down, so the work starts by capturing what the system actually does today — its architecture, its dependencies, the business rules buried in the code — and pinning that behavior down with tests. AI then helps analyze the code, refactor, migrate and write further tests. The engineer reviews every result, catches the wrong assumptions, and makes the calls that carry real risk: what to keep, what to change, and in what order.
